    How does it work?

    Truck tires can become extremely hot during heavy loads, long-distance driving, and high ambient temperatures. Excessive heat can increase the risk of tire failure.

    The DS18B20 measures tire temperature continuously. When the temperature reaches the predefined warning threshold, the Arduino activates a relay that controls an electric valve and starts the cooling process.

    Temperature rises → sensor detects danger → Arduino reacts → valve activates → cooling starts → temperature drops → system returns to SAFE.

    The school prototype uses cold water as a safe demonstration substitute for the proposed cooling medium.
